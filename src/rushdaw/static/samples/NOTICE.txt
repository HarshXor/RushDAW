RushDAW instrument samples
==========================

The recorded guitar and bass notes in this folder come from the
Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont (FluidR3_GM.sf2) by Frank Wen,
as pre-rendered to MP3 by the "MIDI.js Soundfonts" project
(https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Instruments used (one note every 3 semitones; file name = MIDI note number):
  electric_guitar_clean/   Electric Guitar (clean)   MIDI 40-88
  acoustic_guitar_steel/   Acoustic Guitar (steel)   MIDI 40-88
  electric_bass_finger/    Electric Bass (finger)    MIDI 28-67
  electric_bass_pick/      Electric Bass (pick)      MIDI 28-67

The files are unmodified copies; RushDAW re-pitches and processes them at playback.
